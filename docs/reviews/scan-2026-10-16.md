| Excluded source | Reason |
| --- | --- |

The exclusions are recorded with reasons, primary-source links, and a UTC review timestamp in [source-curation.json](../../data/source-curation.json). These three records are omitted from the accepted ledgers and searchable projection. Their original proposed records remain inspectable in the linked PR snapshot.

The background-research delta comprised 49 additions and three updates. All 52 records were checked for identifier/URL consistency, provider provenance, evidence fields, topic fit, declared verification depth, date format, and publication hygiene. Of its 49 additions, 47 were relevant within the stated broad background scope; the two rejected research papers are listed above. Broad model-routing, calibration, and orchestration papers remain background evidence, not direct evidence about Jev's implementation.
