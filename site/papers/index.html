An independent Jev-style decision head on Qwen-Audio-3.1-ASR-Flash-Next gives Choice, Noul and Score readouts over audio, reporting 84.06% on MMSU, a 6.50% speaker-verification equal error rate and about 20× faster inference than its own base model.

Papers · Lan, Kaul, Pandurangan & Sanyal · 2026-10-14 · v1 · Preprint · author-reported

On 47,611 held-out prompt-injection examples, Jev ranks second in AUROC after an LLM judge, its native probabilities do not consistently beat post-hoc calibrated classifiers, and a 1% false-negative target forces over 91.8% escalation in every gate-reviewer system.

Papers · Imperatrice, Kameneva, Greenberg & Romano · 2026-10-14 · v1 · Preprint · author-reported

In 41,500 responses Jev's selected answers look largely unbiased, but its judgments shift with irrelevant numbers and option order, and several bias manipulations move its probabilities even when the answer stays the same; on 30 adapted conjunction tasks it violates the conjunction rule in 54% of 15,000 responses.
